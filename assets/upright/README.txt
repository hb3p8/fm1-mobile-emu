Real pizzicato double-bass attacks, derived from VSCO 2 Community Edition.
https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
https://versilian-studios.com/vsco-community/
Upstream commit: 440300901dfe9275fd84e0b7763af1f8443ae62e

CC0-1.0. Recorded by Sam Gossner and Simon Dalzell (Versilian Studios);
sample cutting by Elan Hickler / Soundemote. Original CC0 dedication included.

Only 96 ms attacks are embedded, in four registers with two alternate takes.
manifest.json records source paths, original hashes, pitches and preparation.
The filenames here use scientific pitch (E1 = MIDI 28); the upstream names
use E0 for the same sounding pitch. Pitch assignments were checked from audio.

The attacks excite the ported Rings string kernel, followed by broad body
resonators. There is no separate synthetic pluck or dry sample mix. Recordings
are not looped as full-note samples. No downloads occur at runtime.
